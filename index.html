Islamic Knowledge Hub

6 category agents, judge loops, fully local on one GPU

A self-hosted agentic RAG system answering questions strictly from a corpus of Islamic books. A gate node classifies and decomposes the query, a router picks the active categories, six lanes retrieve in parallel, and an LLM judge re-queries until the evidence is sufficient — or marks the answer low confidence.

Key Engineering Outcomes
  • Qwen3-14B-AWQ served on a single RTX 3090 Ti with fp8 KV cache and prefix caching
  • bge-M3 embeddings, rerankers and neighbour expansion over metadata-separated books

Patients book by speaking to a realtime voice agent that maps symptoms to the right specialist, checks live availability and writes the booking. Phone, website and front desk share one database, where exclusion constraints make double-booking impossible rather than merely unlikely.
